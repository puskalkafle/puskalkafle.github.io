About

Kathmandu, NP

I'm Puskal — a UX Engineer who lives in the overlap between design and code. I design interfaces, then build them: fast, accessible and pixel-faithful.

The same instinct led me to ship my own: an AI image-upscaling platform with apps on Shopify and WordPress, and a set of production-grade components on the Framer Marketplace.
